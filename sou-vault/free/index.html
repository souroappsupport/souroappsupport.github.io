1Summary#

Souro Vault Go is a private vault that works on your phone, paid for by ads.

What you put in the vault never leaves your phone

It is encrypted on the phone with keys protected by your PIN and your phone's secure hardware. We cannot see it, recover it or reset your PIN. There is no account and no sign-in.

No servers, no analytics of our own

We do not run any servers that receive your data, and the app has no analytics of its own.

Ads come from advertising partners

To show ads, these partners receive some information about your device and how ads are used (section 5). They never receive your vault's contents, your PIN, your files, notes or contacts.

You can turn ads off

Buy “No ads” or Pro through Google Play. The app then makes no ad requests and does not start the advertising software. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

This app is published by SouroApps (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your data, write to souroapps.support@gmail.com.

3What the app handles on your phone#

Everything here stays on your phone, in the app's private storage, unless you move it out yourself (section 8).

3.1What you put in the vault

  • Photos, videos, audio, documents and other files you add.
  • Private notes, private contacts (name, phone number, email, note, photo), and voice or video memos you record in the app.
  • Their names, albums and dates.

All of it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Files are encrypted as they are written, and the app never stores an unencrypted copy of a file. The few short-lived exceptions are deleted when the vault locks: a document open in the PDF viewer, a video while it is being recorded, and a file you choose to open in another app.

3.2Your PIN and biometrics

  • Your PIN is never stored, not even in scrambled form. It is used only at the moment you type it, to unlock your vault's keys.
  • Biometric unlock is handled entirely by Android. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data; Android only tells it whether you were recognised.

3.3Search and on-device AI

  • Content search uses Google ML Kit models built into the app. They look at your vault's photos, PDFs and text files on the phone; nothing is downloaded or sent, and the library's own usage reports are switched off. The results are stored encrypted.
  • Searching the phone (“This device”) reads the phone's own media and the folders you choose, on the phone, and keeps the results in memory only.

3.4Intruder photos and the attempt log (off by default)

  • If you turn on intruder photos, a front-camera photo is taken after several wrong PINs (three by default). It is encrypted and visible only inside your vault.
  • The attempt log records when, where and how many digits were typed – never the digits themselves.
  • Nothing is uploaded.

3.5The everyday tools (calculator, notepad, gallery, music player)

  • These tools show and play the phone's own photos, videos and music, on the phone.
  • What you do in them outside the vault is stored like any ordinary app's data, not encrypted: notepad notes, the calculator's history, gallery and music favourites.
  • Items you move into the vault from these tools are encrypted like everything else in it.

3.6Settings and app state

  • Your settings. Those that would reveal which tool the app opens as are scrambled with a hardware-held key.
  • Your plan (free, No ads or Pro).
  • Counters that time offers and ads: for example, when the last full-screen ad was shown, how many rewarded ads you watched today, and how many days you have used the app.

All of these stay on the phone.

4What we collect#

We – the developer – do not collect personal data. We have no servers that receive information from the app, and we do not sell your information.

The information described in section 5 is collected by the advertising partners, directly from the app, to show ads.

5Advertising#

5.1What the advertising partners receive

When ads are shown to free users, the advertising software in the app (and the ad networks it works with) may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and similar identifiers.
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it (country or city level – the app has no location permission and never reads GPS).
  • Device and app information: phone model, Android version, language, screen size, network type, this app's name and version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed, clicked or watched to the end.
  • Diagnostics: information about how the advertising software performs, such as load times and errors.

They use this information to choose and show ads, to measure them, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to prevent fraud. Depending on your choices (section 5.3), ads may be personalised to your interests.

The advertising partners never receive

  • Anything in your vault
  • Your PIN
  • Your files and photos
  • Your notes
  • Your contacts
  • Your recordings
  • Your search words
  • Anything from the calculator, notepad, gallery or music player

5.3Your choices

  • Consent

    Where the law of your country requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If the app asked you, you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy settings for ads.

  • Advertising ID

    In your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID. Partners then cannot link ads to it.

  • No ads at all

    Buy “No ads” or Pro (section 7). The app then makes no ad requests.

  • No connection

    The app checks whether the phone is online, so that no ad is pushed or offered when one cannot load. This check stays on the phone.

Where ads appear.

Ads can appear

  • Where you browse your lists
  • On the vault's home screen
  • At natural breaks
  • When you choose to watch one for a reward

Ads never appear

  • On the PIN screen
  • In the photo and video viewer
  • In the players, editors, camera or recorder
  • In Settings
  • On the backup screen

5.4Where the information is processed

Our advertising partners are based in several countries – for example the United States, India, China and the United Arab Emirates – and may process information in other countries too. Yandex states that information it collects may be transferred to Russia. These countries may not protect personal data in the same way as the country you live in. Each partner describes its safeguards in its own privacy policy (section 5.2).

5.5Legal bases

Where a law such as the EU or UK GDPR asks for a legal basis:

  • Personalised ads, and storing or reading identifiers on your device for advertising, are based on your consent.
  • Ads that are not personalised, ad measurement and fraud prevention are based on our and our partners' legitimate interest in funding a free app and keeping ads honest.

You can withdraw consent at any time (section 5.3).

5.6“Sharing” and targeted advertising

Under some laws – for example those of California and other US states – sharing device identifiers with advertising partners for personalised ads may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising, or as “targeted advertising”. You can opt out by deleting or resetting your advertising ID (section 5.3), by refusing personalised ads where the app offers that choice, or by writing to us. We do not sell personal information for money.

8When data leaves the vault – only when you do it#

Data leaves the vault only through actions you take:

  • Share an item with another app.

  • Restore items to the phone's ordinary storage. They are then no longer encrypted.

  • Open with another app. A temporary copy is made for that app and deleted when the vault locks.

  • Encrypted backup: a file saved where you choose, encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

  • Export contacts as a .vcf file. It is not encrypted, and the app warns you first.

Files keep the information they already contain (for example a photo's date or location tags) when shared or restored.

9Security#

  • AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Keys protected by your PIN and by your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore).
  • Vault screens cannot be screenshotted.
  • The vault locks when the screen goes off and, unless you change the setting, when you leave the app.

No security is perfect. If you forget your PIN, nobody – including us – can open the vault.

Deleting the original of a file you moved into the vault is done as well as Android allows, but flash storage may keep traces until they are overwritten.

10How long data is kept, and deleting it#

On your phone:

  • everything stays until you delete it;
  • deleted items wait 30 days in the recovery bin unless you empty it;
  • uninstalling deletes all the app's data, including your vault, so make an encrypted backup first if you want to keep it;
  • Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer are switched off for this app.

With advertising partners: each keeps the data it collected for the time its own privacy policy states. You can limit it by deleting your advertising ID, or ask the partner directly. We do not hold that data, so we cannot delete it for them.

11Children#

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children, and ads in the app are not meant for children. If you believe a child has used the app and want data removed from a partner, contact us and we will help you reach them.

We hold no personal data about you. For data held by an advertising partner, use the choices in section 5.3 or contact that partner. We are glad to help – write to souroapps.support@gmail.com.
