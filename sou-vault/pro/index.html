1Summary#

Souro Vault is a private vault that works entirely on your phone.

The app cannot use the internet

It does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

We collect no personal data

There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We have no servers that receive anything from the app.

Your vault is encrypted on your phone

The keys are protected by your PIN and your phone's secure hardware. We cannot see what is in it, recover it or reset your PIN.

Purchases are handled by Google Play

We never receive your payment details.

2Who is responsible#

This app is published by SouroApps (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your data, write to souroapps.support@gmail.com.

3What the app handles – and where it stays#

Everything below is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and never leaves it unless you choose to move it out (section 7).

3.1What you put in the vault

  • Photos, videos, audio, documents and other files you add.
  • Private notes, private contacts (name, phone number, email, note, photo), and voice or video memos you record in the app.
  • The names you give them, the albums you make, and when they were added.

All of it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Files are encrypted as they are written, and the app never stores an unencrypted copy of a file. The few short-lived exceptions are deleted when the vault locks: a document open in the PDF viewer, a video while it is being recorded, and a file you choose to open in another app.

3.2Your PIN and biometrics

  • Your PIN is never stored, not even in scrambled form. It is used only at the moment you type it, to unlock the keys that decrypt your vault.
  • Biometric unlock (fingerprint or face) is handled entirely by Android. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data; Android only tells it whether you were recognised.

3.3Search and on-device AI

If you turn on content search, the app looks at your vault's photos, PDFs and text files on your phone to note what they show (“dog”, “beach”) and the words in them. This uses Google ML Kit models that are built into the app: nothing is downloaded, nothing is sent, and the library's own usage reports are switched off. The results are stored encrypted in the vault.

When you search the phone (“This device”), the app reads the phone's own photos, videos, music and documents (in folders you pick) on the phone, and keeps the results in memory only while the search screen is open.

3.4Intruder photos and the attempt log (off by default)

  • If you turn on intruder photos, the app takes a front-camera photo after several wrong PINs (three by default). The photo is encrypted and can be seen only inside your vault.
  • The attempt log records when a wrong PIN was typed, on which screen, and how many digits it had. It never records the digits themselves.
  • Nothing is uploaded or sent to anyone.

3.5The everyday tools (calculator, notepad, gallery, music player)

The app can look like, and work as, its own calculator, notepad, gallery or music player.

  • These tools show and play the phone's own photos, videos and music. They read them on the phone and do not copy them anywhere.
  • What you do in these tools outside the vault is stored like any ordinary app's data, not encrypted, so the tools look normal:
    • notes written in the notepad;
    • the calculator's history;
    • the favourites you mark in the gallery and the music player.
  • Items you move into the vault from these tools are encrypted like everything else in it.

3.6Settings and app state

  • Your settings: language, look, which tool the app opens as, security options. Settings that would reveal which tool the app uses are stored scrambled with a key held in your phone's secure hardware.
  • Which plan you have (free or Pro), so the app works without a connection.
  • A few counters used to time offers (for example, how many days you have used the app).

4What we collect#

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, no account and no servers of ours behind it. We do not receive, sell, rent or share any information about you or your use of the app.

Google, as the operator of Android and Google Play, may receive information under its own terms – for example, crash and usage statistics that you allowed Android to share, or your purchase history in Google Play. That is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not by this policy.

6Purchases#

  • Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and we never see your card or payment details.
  • The app receives only Google Play's confirmation of what this Google account has bought. It checks it on the phone and remembers which plan you have.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription, use Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

7When data leaves the vault – only when you do it#

Data leaves the vault only through actions you take:

  • Share an item with another app.

  • Restore items to the phone's ordinary storage. They are then no longer encrypted.

  • Open with another app. A temporary copy is made for that app and deleted when the vault locks.

  • Encrypted backup: a backup file saved where you choose. It is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, which we never know.

  • Export contacts as a .vcf file. It is not encrypted, and the app tells you so first.

Files keep whatever information they already contain – for example, a photo's date or location tags – when you share or restore them.

8Security#

  • AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • The keys are protected in two layers: your PIN, and a key in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore) that cannot be copied off the phone.
  • Screens inside the vault cannot be screenshotted or recorded.
  • The vault locks when the screen goes off and, unless you change the setting, when you leave the app.

No security is perfect. If you forget your PIN, your vault cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

Deleting the original of a file you moved into the vault is done as well as Android allows, but flash storage may keep traces until they are overwritten.

9How long data is kept, and deleting it#

  • Everything stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Items you delete go to the recovery bin for 30 days, unless you empty it sooner.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data, including your vault. Make an encrypted backup first if you want to keep it.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer are switched off for this app, so no copy of your data is made there.
  • Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.
